            Update KYC Documents

            For physical shareholders:

            In order to better serve the investors, the exchange has signed up with its RTA, KFin Technologies Limited to provide an on-line facility for activities like, raising Service Request, Queries, Complaints, check the status, KYC details, Dividend, eMeeting and eVoting details.

            This online application can be accessed at RTA’s website > Investor Services > Investor Support. Members are required to register / signup, using their Name, PAN, Mobile and email ID. Post registration, user can login via OTP. Quick link to access the signup page at RTA’s website.

            Under this service, the facility to upload KYC documents will be available only for shareholders holding shares in physical mode. Shareholders holding shares in Demat mode are required to approach their Depository Participants as per the extant guidelines issued in this regard for updating their KYC details which will be updated in the records of the Company/RTA.

            Facility To Update KYC For MCX Shareholders

            For shareholders holding shares in Demat mode:

            All shareholders are required to comply with the Know Your Client (KYC) norms under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act 2002.

            Shareholders have to update their KYC through their Depository Participant. In case the KYC is not updated, the shareholders are requested to update their KYC. Please submit a duly filled in KYC form provided by the Depository Participant, along with the documents specified therein. Individual shareholders should produce a Proof of identity (Photo copy PAN card, passport, driving license, etc.) and Proof of Address. Non - Individual Shareholders will have to produce documents pertaining to its constitution/registration to fulfil the KYC process. In case of joint accounts, separate forms need to be filled by the joint holders. Self attested copy of Aadhaar card has to be attached for Aadhaar number updation.
